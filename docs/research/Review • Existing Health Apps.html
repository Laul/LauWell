Profiles

Structure, visual approach, and the single thing worth taking from each. The two panels beside every app are schematic reconstructions of its home screen and its second surface — real palette, header treatment, nav pattern and card rhythm. Samsung Health, Guava, Human Health, Bearable, Exist, Heads Up and OstoBuddy were redrawn after looking at the live apps and store galleries directly, which corrected several earlier guesses; Apple, Google Health, Fasten and Nomie are still reconstructed from documentation. They are diagrams, not screenshots — the links on each card open the real galleries.

Confidence note. Seven of the eleven were checked against the live product or store gallery, which corrected real errors: Bearable is light-themed with an indigo header, not dark; Guava has four destinations, not three; and OstoBuddy already carries structured cross-brand product references, so the catalogue gap is narrower than first stated. Four remain reconstructions from documentation. Apple Health's structure comes from iOS 27.2 beta coverage this month and may shift before release. Google Health launched in May 2026, is US-only, and its internal navigation is thinly documented — its panels are the least certain here. Fasten's interface is inferred from its self-hosted nature. Nomie's site is now only a shutdown notice, so its panels come from the open-source version. Treat the matrix as directional, not audited.
